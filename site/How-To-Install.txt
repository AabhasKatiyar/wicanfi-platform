================================================================
 WiCanFi — Fast 1-Minute Chrome & Edge Setup Guide
================================================================

Step 1: Open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and navigate to:
        chrome://extensions   (Google Chrome)
        edge://extensions     (Microsoft Edge)

Step 2: Turn ON "Developer mode" (toggle switch in the top-right corner).

Step 3: Click "Load unpacked" (button in the top-left corner).

Step 4: Select the "WiCanFi-Extension" folder.

That's it! WiCanFi is installed and will automatically handle your campus Wi-Fi.

----------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO UPDATE ANYTIME:
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Windows: Double-click Update-Windows.bat
- Apple Mac: Double-click Update-Mac.command
  (Or in Terminal: curl -fsSL https://wicanfi.pages.dev/update-mac.sh | bash)

----------------------------------------------------------------
APPLE MAC NOTE (If Mac says "Not Opened / Apple could not verify... Move to Bin")
----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Why does macOS show "Update-Mac.command Not Opened"?
A: macOS Gatekeeper automatically places all downloaded internet files into "Quarantine".
   Because WiCanFi is a free open-source student tool and not signed with an
   expensive Apple Developer ID, macOS Sequoia and Sonoma block unsigned scripts by default.

HOW TO RUN ON MAC (3 Easy Methods):

  METHOD 1 (Terminal 1-Liner — FASTEST, 100% RELIABLE & ZERO WARNINGS):
  Open Terminal (Press Cmd + Space, type "Terminal", hit Enter) and paste:
  curl -fsSL https://wicanfi.pages.dev/update-mac.sh | bash
  (This downloads, unblocks Gatekeeper automatically, and opens the updated folder!)

  METHOD 2 (System Settings — macOS 15 Sequoia & macOS 14 Sonoma):
  1. On the "Not Opened" popup dialog, click "Done" (DO NOT click "Move to Bin"!).
  2. Click Apple Menu () in top-left -> System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the "Security" section.
  4. You will see: '"Update-Mac.command" was blocked to protect your Mac.'
  5. Click "Open Anyway", enter your Mac password / Touch ID, and click "Open".
     (After doing this once, it permanently unblocks and works forever!)

  METHOD 3 (Right-click on macOS Ventura and earlier):
  1. Right-Click (or Control + Click) "Update-Mac.command".
  2. Select "Open" from the menu, then click "Open" in the dialog box.

----------------------------------------------------------------
WINDOWS SECURITY NOTE (Why "Unknown Publisher" appears & Is it safe?)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Why does Windows SmartScreen say "Windows protected your PC" or 
   "Unknown Publisher" when I run Update-Windows.bat?

A: WiCanFi is a verified open-source, non-profit campus tool:
   https://github.com/AabhasKatiyar/wicanfi

   Microsoft Windows Defender SmartScreen automatically displays this warning
   on ANY batch file (.bat) downloaded from the internet because .bat scripts
   cannot hold commercial EV corporate certificates (which cost $500/year).

   WiCanFi is 100% safe, contains zero spyware, ads, or background trackers.
   Because it is a standard batch script, you can right-click Update-Windows.bat
   and click "Edit" in Notepad to inspect every line of code yourself!

TWO EASY WAYS TO RUN WITHOUT WORRY:

  METHOD 1 (Takes 1 second):
  1. On the blue "Windows protected your PC" window, click "More info".
  2. Click "Run anyway". The updater will run safely and unblock future runs.

  METHOD 2 (Permanent zero-warning fix before unzipping):
  1. Right-click the downloaded "WiCanFi-Extension.zip" file.
  2. Click "Properties".
  3. At the bottom under Security, check the [x] "Unblock" box.
  4. Click "Apply" and "OK", then extract the folder.
     Windows will never show any warnings!
