The Core Problem & Solution

Why Campus Wi-Fi Gets Frustrating

Entering your credentials on a captive portal is annoying enough. But the real friction happens after authentication when that login tab is gone.

⚠️

Connected to Wi-Fi. Logged in. Closed the portal. Now what?

Campus Wi-Fi networks rely on captive portals. You connect to your university Wi-Fi, open a browser tab, enter your student credentials, and authenticate. But the moment you close that login tab, you're left with an active session and zero convenient ways to control it:

1
Ghost Active Sessions: You close the captive portal tab to focus on work. Your campus session remains active in the network background with no visible status.
2
Device Limit Lockouts: Most university networks allow only one active device at a time. With your laptop session still running, you cannot authenticate your phone or tablet.
3
No Easy Way to Sign Out: Once the portal tab is closed, there is no obvious link or bookmark to end your session. You're left hunting for obscure local IP addresses.
4
Manual Re-Discovery: When a session drops or times out during class, you must manually trigger or wait for a captive prompt, dig through history, or restart your browser.
🛡️

WiCanFi keeps your campus session under control.

WiCanFi acts as a lightweight campus Wi-Fi control layer in your browser. It automates your official campus portal login and keeps session controls at your fingertips—even when the portal tab has been closed.

⚡

Automatic Authentication

Detects the campus network and completes the official captive portal login using your locally stored student credentials.

📊

Live Session Status

Shows whether your campus session is active in real time from your toolbar so you never have to guess your connection state.

🔄

1-Click Re-Authentication

If your session drops or expires, re-authenticate immediately from your browser toolbar without searching for the portal URL.

🚪

Easy Session Sign Out

Sign out of your own session in one click to free up your campus Wi-Fi allowance for your phone, tablet, or another device.

🎯

Official Portal Fallback

If direct communication is unavailable or fails, WiCanFi immediately opens the official campus portal so you're never stuck.

🔒

Credentials Stored Locally

Zero-knowledge privacy. Your student ID and password stay encrypted in your local browser sandbox—never on any server.

Core User Flow

How WiCanFi Works

WiCanFi does not hack, bypass, or circumvent your university network. It automates your normal authentication workflow and provides a reliable control layer for your session.

Step 1 · Connect
1

Connect

Connect to your campus Wi-Fi normally from Windows or macOS. WiCanFi detects the campus network and verifies gateway reachability.

Step 2 · Authenticate
2

Authenticate

WiCanFi handles the official portal login using your locally saved credentials, completing authentication without manual typing.

Step 3 · Control
3

Control

Close the portal tab and get to work. Use WiCanFi from your toolbar whenever you need to check session status, re-authenticate, or sign out.

Quick Setup

Ready in Less Than 60 Seconds

No complicated developer tools or terminal commands required. Choose your browser below and follow the four quick steps:

1

Download & Extract

Download WiCanFi-Extension.zip and extract the archive to any folder on your laptop.

2

Open Extension Manager

3

Toggle Developer Mode

Turn ON the Developer mode switch located in the top-right corner.

4

Load Unpacked

Click "Load unpacked" in the top-left and select the extracted folder.
