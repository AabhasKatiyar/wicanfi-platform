Is WiCanFi Safe? (Windows "Unknown Publisher" Notice)

Why Windows SmartScreen flags open-source update scripts and how your security is guaranteed

🛡️ 100% Open-Source

When you download and run Update-Windows.bat, Windows Defender SmartScreen may display a blue dialog stating: "Windows protected your PC — Publisher: Unknown publisher".

❓ Why does Windows show this?

Windows batch files (.bat) are plain text scripts and cannot technically hold digital certificates. Microsoft also requires commercial corporations to purchase $500/year "Extended Validation" certificates. Free student projects do not have corporate certificate contracts, so Windows labels all downloaded batch files as "Unknown".

✓ Complete Transparency & Safety

WiCanFi is open-source under the MIT license. Every single line of Update-Windows.bat is open and inspectable in Notepad. It contains zero binary exes, zero telemetry tracking of passwords, and zero third-party adware.

⚡ Two ways to run without seeing warnings:
  • Quick run: On the blue Windows SmartScreen prompt, click More info ➔ Run anyway. The updater will automatically unblock future updates!
  • Permanent unblock: Right-click WiCanFi-Extension.zip before extracting ➔ Click Properties ➔ Check the "Unblock" box at the bottom ➔ Click OK. All files will open with zero warnings!
